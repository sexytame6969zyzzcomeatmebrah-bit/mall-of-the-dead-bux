CO-OP LOBBY

HOW TO PLAY

Survive the waves. Zombies pour in through the four gates. Every 5th wave is a boss: the Giant, then Zombie Santa.

PC: WASD move · mouse aim · click shoot · R reload · Q, wheel or 1-6 switch guns · G / right-click grenade · Shift or Space dash · E use doors and the radio, hold E next to a downed friend to revive.

Phone: left thumb anywhere to move, right thumb anywhere to aim and fire. Buttons for dash, grenade, reload, gun and use.

Gamepad: sticks move and aim · RT fire · A dash · RB grenade · X reload · Y gun · B use.

Loot: kill the glowing crate carriers to unlock new guns for the whole team. Zombies sometimes drop power-ups.

Perks: after each wave pick 1 of 3 perks (keys 1-3 or tap).

Watch for: crawlers (small, fast), bloaters (explode), spitters (acid), riot zombies (shielded from the front), red barrels (shoot them).

Nick's Office: close the door with E for a breather. They will break it down.
